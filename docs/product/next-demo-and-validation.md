## First audience: hypothesis to validate

Primary daily users: Indian TPA cashless pre-authorisation processors and medical
officers. First buyer/design-partner sponsor: a TPA cashless operations head,
claims operations leader or COO, with medical/IT/security stakeholders.

Hospital insurance/TPA desks are useful adjacent interviewees about incomplete
requests and repeated queries. They are not automatically the buyer or a reason
to pivot to a hospital-facing product. Patients are beneficiaries, not the
initial target customer. Do not sell autonomous authorisation/denial.

Initial proposition: an evidence-first reviewer workspace that brings policy
clauses, member context and deterministic rupee calculations together while a
human retains the decision. Whether this is differentiated and worth buying is
unproven; interviews must test it.
